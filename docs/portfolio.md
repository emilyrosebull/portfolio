## League: New Hire Onboarding Overview
**Type:** Conceptual overview / process diagram
**Audience:** New employees enrolling in benefits for the first time

Designed a swimlane diagram to map the end-to-end onboarding journey from hire date to active benefits. The diagram replaced what would have been a long written explanation, reducing cognitive load for an audience navigating an unfamiliar process under time pressure.

## League: Reporting Qualifying Life Events
**Type:** Complex end-user process
**Audience:** Benefits members experiencing a life change (such as marriage or a new dependent)

Documented a multi-path workflow covering a high-stakes, time-sensitive process. The article handles branching logic across mobile and web platforms, conditional outcomes based on HR approval settings, and edge cases like reporting outside the eligibility window, while remaining clear to a non-technical audience under stress.

[View article →](https://help.league.com/en/faqs/Working-version/CAN/reporting-qualifying-life-events-qles)

## League: View, Download, and Use Your Digital Benefits Cards
**Type:** Simple end-user process
**Audience:** Employees accessing benefits coverage without a physical benefits ID card

Documented a straightforward but practically important task: accessing digital ID cards in time-sensitive situations like a dental appointment. Structured parallel instructions for mobile and web, and provided just enough context on unfamiliar terms (Group ID, Member ID) without over-explaining.
